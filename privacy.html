Privacy and limitations

Room to Breathe is a static storage-guidance app. It has no account, analytics SDK, advertising code, server database or upload endpoint.

What stays on your iPhone

Storage measurements, the WhatsApp backup status you select, and your recovery history are saved in your browser's local storage. Photos and videos you explicitly select are inspected for file metadata (names and sizes) in the browser, but not uploaded, saved or deleted by the app.

What public hosting means

The source code of this site is public. GitHub Pages and related network infrastructure may collect ordinary access information such as IP addresses and request details. Pages hosted under the same account's github.io hostname share the same browser origin: code on another site under that hostname could potentially access this app's local storage. Do not enter secrets or confidential documents.

Your control

Export or erase your locally stored readings in the app's settings. Clearing website storage, changing browsers or using another iPhone may lose your records. The app cannot read your iPhone's system-wide storage automatically and cannot inspect or delete WhatsApp data, Photos library items or any other app's private files.

Safety

Backups are a safeguard, not a permanent archive of subsequently deleted media. You must personally review and approve any deletions inside the official apps. Recovery progress is calculated from the iPhone Storage figures you enter and can fluctuate due to iOS system activity.
